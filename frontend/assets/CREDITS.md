# Texture credits

The 3D preview's surface textures (`textures/<finish>/{albedo,normal,roughness}.jpg`) come from
[ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com), bundled here so the app works offline.

Licence: [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/) (public domain, no attribution required).
Fetched: 2026-10-26, the 1K-JPG download of each set; normals are the OpenGL (`NormalGL`) maps.

Changes from the originals: albedo is desaturated (the design's colour tints it in the app) and, like roughness,
rescaled to a linear mean of 0.5 with its contrast reduced; normal maps are flattened for the subtler surfaces;
laminate, metal, steel, plastic and ceramic are downsized to 512 px.
